    How to play

    Start with 10 health and 20 gold. Explore an endless dungeon and win gold at blackjack. There is no final boss. Your run ends when health reaches zero or you cannot afford the next hand’s ante. The gold you have left is your final score, recorded with up to three initials in the top 10 on this browser and device.

    Every hand costs an ante. The monster matches it: win and collect twice your ante (a net gain of one ante), lose and your ante is gone plus you take damage, tie and your ante returns. Aces count as 1 or 11; face cards count as 10. A natural blackjack beats an ordinary 21. Hit takes a card; Stand lets the monster play. Its second card stays hidden until the hand ends.

    Each winning hand removes one monster heart. Defeat a monster and keep exploring. Monsters can return, with higher levels appearing as you defeat more enemies. Higher levels bring larger antes, more endurance and tougher playing rules. The ante is always visible before the next hand.

    Chests contain gold or Identify potions. Carry multiple Identify potions and use one in battle to reveal that enemy’s level and exact playing behavior. That monster type and level remain known for the rest of this run; others say “Not identified.” Identification does not reveal hidden cards. A new run resets your inventory and knowledge.

    Exploration now reveals different full-room pixel-art scenes. Random fountains restore up to 3 health; abandoned camps restore 1. Some chambers only hold eerie echoes. Hidden darts cost 1 health. A cursed altar offers a choice: leave safely, or take a 50% chance of gaining 6 gold; failure costs 2 health and can end the run. Chests still contain gold or Identify potions. Between encounters you can spend 5 gold to heal up to 4 health, trading potential score for survival. Your run saves on this browser when storage is available. This version starts a fresh run under the new rules; older demo saves are kept separately.

    Sound: Power On plays the title theme. Start Adventure begins your run. Exploration and battle have their own original chiptune loops, with victory fanfares and hit, hurt, card, treasure and healing effects. The Sound button mutes everything. Audio pauses when you leave the tab.

    Keyboard: E = Explore / Next hand, H = Hit, S = Stand, I = Identify. Fictional game gold only; no real money.
