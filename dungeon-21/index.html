        How to play

        Explore and Status appear while exploring; Hit and Stand appear in battle. Inventory is near the top. Status shows your current run’s progress, supplies, hand results and more. Currency is G: -1G ante is a debit, +2G returned is a payout. Health uses ♥: -1♥ is damage and +4♥ is healing. Results use coloured text without highlight boxes.

        The king sends you into the Halls of the Obsidian Veil with 10 health and just 5 gold. Explore Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire and Amethyst Halls. Buy one key in each hall and find its sealed stair: Red 25 gold, Blue 50, Gold 75, Amethyst 100. The last key reveals the final chamber, its boss and the secret.

        Each hall has a unique monster roster; deeper halls have higher antes, more hearts, more damage and smarter play. More kinds of monsters appear as you defeat enemies in that hall. The ante is always visible. Win a hand to remove one monster heart and collect twice your stake. Losing costs your stake and health. Ties refund the stake. Aces count as 1 or 11; face cards as 10. A natural blackjack beats an ordinary 21.

        After a loss, try again for the next ante or spend 2 gold to run. Zero health, or being unable to afford another hand or escape, ends the quest. Remaining gold is your score. Save up to three initials locally. Admin test runs are excluded from ranking.

        Rooms: battles 45%, chests 18%, shops 12%, sealed stairs 8%, fountains 5%, cursed altars 4%, camps 3%, traps 3%, eerie chambers 2%. A shop is guaranteed by the eighth exploration without one, overriding the ordinary roll. Fountains restore up to 3 health, camps 1. Altars offer a stated 50% chance of +6 gold versus 2 damage. Traps cost 1 health.

        Shops sell this hall’s key, Identify and healing potions for 5 gold each. You begin with an empty inventory. Use Inventory to drink a healing potion (up to 4 health) or identify the current enemy. Using a healing potion asks for confirmation and consumes one potion; it does not spend more gold. Identify reveals an enemy’s level and play rules, remembered by type, level and hall for the quest. You can carry multiple potions. Descending carries your health, gold, inventory and knowledge forward.

        Each descent reveals a shared password for the next hall. Enter it from the title screen to start there with 10 health and 5 gold. The Emerald password starts in the first hall. Admin codes are Gold, Goldruby, Goldsapphire and Goldamethyst; they start with 1,000 gold in the named hall. Codes ignore capitalization. Entering a code replaces the current quest only after confirmation if one is underway.

        This quest uses a separate save from the older endless demo. Earlier saves and local scores remain on your device. Power On enables the original soundtrack; Sound mutes everything. Audio pauses when you leave the tab.

        Keyboard: E = Explore / next hand, H = Hit, S = Stand, I = Inventory, Escape = close Inventory / Status, R = Run after a loss. Fictional game gold only; no real money.
