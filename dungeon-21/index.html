How to play

Explore the dungeon and beat monsters at blackjack. Get closer to 21 than your opponent without going over. Aces count as 1 or 11; face cards count as 10. Hit takes a card. Stand ends your turn. The monster's second card stays hidden until you stand.

Win a hand to remove one monster heart. Lose a hand and take damage. Ties cause no damage. A natural blackjack beats an ordinary 21. Weak monsters make foolish decisions; stronger enemies play more carefully and need multiple wins.

Fountains heal you, gold buys a potion between encounters, and victories grant experience. Defeat all five monsters to clear the dungeon. Your run saves on this device when storage is available.

Keyboard: E = Explore / Next hand, H = Hit, S = Stand. No wagering or real money.
