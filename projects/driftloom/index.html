A little music. A whole world.

Driftloom explores a collaborative way to make music: choose a style, explore chords, play a progression, then develop it into a finished arrangement and a world of animated art.

First creation: Orbital Drift

A 3:19 space ambient composition built from Steve’s chord choices: Am9 → G6 → Fmaj7 → Em7. At 82 BPM, each chord floats for 16 beats. Soft pads gradually give way to deep bass and scattered shimmering notes before settling into a quiet ending.

The visual world — planned

A slow journey through a living nebula. Stars emerge from darkness, translucent rings drift into view, and glowing threads follow gentle orbits through clouds of colour. The scene will evolve with the musical phrases and dissolve into a distant glow.

Taking shape

  • An immersive listening gallery, beginning with Orbital Drift.
  • A chord-playing studio with a 4 × 4 grid, sound choices, volume, tempo and a gentle metronome.
  • Animated artwork shaped around each composition.
  • Future recording tools to capture chord choices and performance timing directly.

The chord-player prototype and first composition have been created. The public listening experience and animation are still being developed.

What’s new

Driftloom joins the North Path collection in early development, with its first composition and visual direction established.
