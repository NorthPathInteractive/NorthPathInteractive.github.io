The Vaults of Velora

Wash ashore on Velora, choose a hero and explore a mysterious island in this early Commodore 64 RPG prototype.

Status
In development · archived prototype
Build
FULL-PROTOTYPE-v2 · archived build
Platform
Commodore 64 · C64 BASIC

Inside the project

  • Three heroes: Toren Vask, Rhea Sallow and Maren Voss.
  • Beach exploration, a live HUD and Cave Goblin encounters.
  • Strike, Guard and Maneuver combat choices.

Try the archived builds

Download a build and open it in a Commodore 64 emulator or transfer it to compatible hardware. Browser play is not yet configured.

  • VELORA-FULL-PROTOTYPE-v2.prg
  • VELORA-MAP-TEST.prg

Controls

W/A/S/D to move; Q to quit. Choose a hero with 1–3 and use 1–3 in combat.

Related project: Asterra Engine, the reusable foundation proposed for this adventure.

Development notes

The full prototype is recovered from the uploaded archive. It has been inspected as BASIC program data, but has not yet passed emulator playtesting. This is not a complete adventure.
