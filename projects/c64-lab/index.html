C64 Lab

A browser playground for Commodore 64 RPGs. Explore recovered adventures, engine experiments and the worlds still taking shape.

Status
In development
Player version
v0.1.4
Format
C64 browser emulator

Small machines. Unfinished worlds.

A place to revisit old builds and try new ideas on a machine with just 64 KB of memory. C64 Lab brings the recovered programs together in one browser player, powered by VirtualC64Web.

Open C64 Lab ↗

Inside the lab

  • Moon Moth: a new neon garden game. Gather moonlight, drift through wrapping gardens and avoid wandering shadows. Play Moon Moth.
  • The Vaults of Velora: an early RPG prototype with three heroes, beach exploration and Cave Goblin combat. A separate map test focuses on tiles and movement.
  • Asterra Engine: a small test world with movement, a wandering NPC and combat, plus a visibility experiment that prints a wall render list.
  • GORK 1: explore a generated maze, collect gold, find the golden key and escape the Forgotten Vault.

How to explore

Choose a build in the lab, then select Start. Use a physical keyboard or the on-screen controls. Each build has its own instructions, and the full C64 keyboard is available for extra keys. You can also download the programs for a C64 emulator or compatible hardware.

These are development builds, with rough edges and unfinished content. Restarting discards the current run. The player version describes the browser lab; individual games retain their original build labels.
