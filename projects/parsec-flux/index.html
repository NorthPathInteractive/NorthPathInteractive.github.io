Jump the sound

Listen as a glassy tone moves toward the center of your headphones. Tap just before it arrives, or press Space or Enter, to jump over it. Clear three incoming tones to complete the chord and enter the next world.

  • Eight distinct sound worlds, beginning with Bloom and its warm Cmaj9 atmosphere.
  • Rich sampled pads and four-chord progressions that change every 16 beats.
  • Each cleared sound adds another tone to your chord. A miss costs a life and resets the harmony to its root.
  • Vocoded world introductions and remaining-life cues, including “One life remaining.”
  • A three-second rising triad fanfare celebrates each completed chord before the next world begins.
  • Touch and keyboard controls, with adjustable game volume.

What’s new in v0.70

Jump over sounds while airborne to clear them. The new jump arc and takeoff cue reward timing; jumping too early or too late costs a life. Repeated taps and held keys do not extend a jump.

The latest build brings together all eight sampled soundscapes, evolving chord progressions, robot announcements, life cues and the rising completion fanfare.

Current development build: v0.70. Best played with headphones.
